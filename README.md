An app that helps you find the files you missed in that mess on your computer.

It uses a system to save your sessions on your PC in order. No security is breached, and no data is sent or altered.
Pan runs entirely on your device. No data ever leaves your computer.
No cloud, no telemetry, no accounts. Pan reads file metadata only — names, paths, sizes, and timestamps.
It never reads the contents of your files.

Press ⌘+Option+P (Mac) or Ctrl+Alt+P (Windows) from anywhere on your computer. Pan appears instantly, ready to search.

New features are coming, the current release is a draft.
